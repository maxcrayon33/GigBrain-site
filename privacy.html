Privacy policy

Your gig library is private by design.

Last updated 11 August 2026

The short version

GigBrain is built to work on your device. There are no accounts or GigBrain servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data. Your gig library stays on your device unless you export or share it; the only optional network request sends the place names you choose to Apple Maps after you tap for a journey estimate.

What GigBrain stores

Everything you add—including gigs, plans, ticket information, travel and stay details, spending, ratings, recaps, notes, setlists, companions and attached images—is saved in GigBrain's local database on your device. It is not uploaded to GigBrain.

Calendar

Calendar access is optional and requested only when you choose to import an event or export a gig. Imported events are stored locally like other gigs. GigBrain does not read your calendar in the background.

Photos and text recognition

When you add artwork or import a screenshot, you select images through Apple's system photo picker. Only images you specifically choose are copied into the app. GigBrain cannot browse the rest of your photo library, and text recognition is performed on your device.

Notifications and widgets

If enabled, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Widgets receive a limited local snapshot through the app's shared container so they can display upcoming and archive information. This data is not sent to GigBrain.

Apple Maps

Journey estimates are optional and requested only after you tap to look one up. GigBrain sends the journey's place names to Apple's Maps service and receives an estimated travel time. GigBrain does not retain or receive your precise location, and no copy is sent to GigBrain or any service other than Apple Maps.

Analytics and tracking

GigBrain contains no advertising, third-party analytics, tracking SDKs or use of advertising identifiers.

Backups and sharing

Backups and exports are created only when you request them and saved to a location you choose. Once you move or share an exported file, it is governed by the service or location where you put it.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual gigs or remove your full library from GigBrain's settings. Deleting the app also removes its locally stored data, subject to the normal behaviour of any device backups you manage outside GigBrain.

Contact

For privacy questions, visit GigBrain Support.
