When reporting a problem

Please include:

  • What you were trying to do
  • What happened instead
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • A screenshot, if it does not contain private ticket or personal information

Protect your information

Do not include ticket barcodes, booking references, home addresses or exported backup files in a public support request.
